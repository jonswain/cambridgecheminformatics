Services

From molecules to models to production

I'm a synthetic chemist turned data scientist, specialising in cheminformatics and AI-driven drug discovery. I also bring the same data science toolkit to other fields, particularly health and medical technology.

AI & ML for drug discovery

Molecular property and ADMET prediction, DNA-encoded library analysis, generative de novo design, and methods for searching ultra-large chemical libraries.

Python & cheminformatics tooling

Bespoke, well-tested RDKit-based packages and tools built around your science, automating the workflows your chemists repeat every day.

Deployment & data engineering

Models delivered as production systems: APIs and web apps, containerised and deployed to the cloud with CI/CD and infrastructure as code.

Model evaluation & statistics

Rigorous, reproducible benchmarking, so you know how far to trust a model, plus the data cleaning, visualisation and statistics behind good decisions.

Training & development

Hands-on training that upskills chemists and lab researchers in Python, machine learning and cheminformatics.

About

Hi, I'm Jon

I started my career at the bench, with a PhD in chemistry from the University of Cambridge and postdoctoral work on natural product synthesis, before moving into cheminformatics and machine learning at Vernalis Research and Veriphi. Alongside my work at OpenADMET, building open-source tools for ADMET modelling, I consult through Cambridge Cheminformatics Consulting.

Whether you need predictive models, automated analysis pipelines, or tools that put models into chemists' hands, I focus on practical, well-tested solutions and honest evaluation, so you can act on the results with confidence.
