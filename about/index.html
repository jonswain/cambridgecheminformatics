About

Dr Jonathan Swain

Cheminformatician and data scientist with a PhD in chemistry and hands-on synthetic research experience.

I work across molecular property and ADMET prediction, DNA-encoded library screening, generative de novo design, and ultra-large chemical space search. I deliver models and tooling as production systems, used by medicinal chemists on live drug discovery projects.

I'm an open-source contributor to RDKit and OpenADMET, and I care about rigorous, reproducible model evaluation, so you know how much to trust a model before acting on it. Before data science, I spent years as a synthetic and physical organic chemist. I understand the questions chemists are asking and the data they work with.

I also teach. I lecture regularly at the University of Auckland, was a lecturer and mentor on the AI4Life bootcamp, and I'm an Associate Fellow of the Higher Education Academy.
